## 2. Verdict

The review found **no design errors** in UC-005: all 5 items are consistency/propagation drift against already-confirmed decisions (D-020 ownership, the created first-sight carve-out, the API-002/004 `Submission`/`Result` split) plus two mechanical traceability repairs (WC-04, WC-05). **WC-01 is the substantive one** — the C1→C2 `version_id` split (D-020) is exactly the kind of ownership boundary the derivation discipline must keep clean **before Phase 3 derives `LSP-*`/`ODB-*` from the `SCR-*` rows**.

**No new user decisions required** — every fix applies already-confirmed decisions (D-020, D-016, API-002/004, §5.1); nothing is deferred.

## 3. Positive observations (no action)

- The `WATCHED_FILES` Request maps cleanly to the existing API surface (API-001/004, §4.1, §5.1 `SyncPayload`) — **no new operation** was needed; the `changed`/`created` split reuses the `didChangeWatchedFiles` contract.
- D-020 (state-relative `version_id`) and D-016 (stale-marking) are applied consistently with UC-002/UC-003/UC-004: C1 forwards the raw fact, C2 owns the record.
- The boundary contrast with UC-003 (open document) and UC-004 (close) is stated explicitly, and the planned siblings UC-006/UC-007 are **flagged** rather than half-specified.
